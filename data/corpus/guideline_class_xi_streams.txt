CLASS XI STREAM ALLOCATION GUIDELINE

Issued: 10/02/2026

Seats in Class XI are divided among science, commerce and arts. At least 40 per cent of Class XI seats in a school must be offered in the science stream.

A candidate must have passed the Madhyamik examination of West Bengal Board of Secondary Education or an equivalent examination to apply for Class XI.

Schools of the Higher Secondary Council such as New Barrackpore Colony Boys' High School in New Barrackpore may add a vocational stream with permission.
