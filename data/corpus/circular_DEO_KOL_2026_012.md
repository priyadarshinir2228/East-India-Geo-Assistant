# Circular DEO/KOL/2026/012

Issued by: District Education Office, Kolkata
Date: 20 February 2026
Subject: Lottery and EWS reservation in CBSE and CISCE schools
Applies to: schools affiliated to CBSE and CISCE

25 per cent of entry-level seats are reserved for children from economically weaker sections under the Right to Education Act.

Where applications exceed the number of seats, admission to entry-level seats shall be decided by a public lottery.

The lottery for 2026 will be held on 16 March 2026. This circular applies in particular to Delhi Public School, Nava Nalanda, Albany Hall School and SXCS. Sunrise Public School in Behala has also applied for recognition and will follow the same rule.
