# Circular DEO/KOL/2025/031

Issued by: District Education Office, Kolkata
Date: 10 November 2025
Subject: Class XI admission schedule 2026
Applies to: schools affiliated to WBBSE and WBCHSE

Online application forms for Class XI admission open on 20 May 2026 and close on 5 June 2026. The first merit list will be published on 15 June 2026.

A candidate must have passed the Madhyamik examination of West Bengal Board of Secondary Education or an equivalent examination to apply for Class XI.

Schools such as Hare School and Baranagar RKM High School must display the schedule on their notice boards.
