# Circular DEO/KOL/2026/004

Issued by: District Education Office, Kolkata
Date: 15 January 2026
Subject: Class I admission age limits
Applies to: all WBBSE primary sections

This circular sets the age limits for admission to Class I in the 2026 session.

- The minimum age for Class I is 6 years as on 31 March 2026.
- The maximum age for Class I is 8 years as on the same date.

Heads of schools must verify the birth certificate before confirming admission. Parents in Behala and Garia may contact the sub-inspector of schools for clarifications.
