# Circular DEO/KOL/2026/021

Issued by: District Education Office, Kolkata
Date: 20 April 2026
Subject: Jessore Road closure and school transport
Applies to: all schools in the Dum Dum circle

Because of flyover repair work, Jessore Road will be partly closed between 1 May 2026 and 31 May 2026.

School buses for Dum Dum Krishna Kumar Hindu Academy, Saroj Basu Smriti Balika Vidyamandir and Auxilium Convent must use VIP Road during this period. Admission counselling at Subhas Nagar High School will continue as scheduled.
