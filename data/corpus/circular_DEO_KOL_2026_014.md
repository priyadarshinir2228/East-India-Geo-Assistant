# Circular DEO/KOL/2026/014

Issued by: District Education Office, Kolkata
Date: 5 March 2026
Subject: Revised Class XI admission schedule 2026
Applies to: schools affiliated to WBBSE and WBCHSE

This circular supersedes circular DEO/KOL/2025/031.

Because of the revised board result date, online application forms for Class XI admission now open on 25 May 2026 and close on 10 June 2026. The first merit list will be published on 20 June 2026.

All other conditions of the earlier circular remain in force.
