# Circular DEO/DJL/2026/002

Issued by: District Education Office, Darjeeling
Date: 25 January 2026
Subject: Hill schools admission dates
Applies to: hill schools affiliated to WBBSE and CISCE

Admission tests for the hill schools of Chowrasta and North Point will be held on 14 February 2026. The new session begins on 2 March 2026.

This applies to Loretto Convent, St. Paul's School, Mount Hermon School and Maharani Girls' High School.
