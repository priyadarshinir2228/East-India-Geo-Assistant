# Admission Guidelines 2026 (Kolkata district)

These guidelines summarise the admission rules for the 2026 session, issued on 10 January 2026.

## Age

The minimum age for Class I is 6 years as on 31 March 2026. The maximum age for Class I is 8 years as on the same date.

## Selection

Where applications exceed the number of seats, admission to entry-level seats shall be decided by a public lottery. WBBSE schools publish merit lists for Class XI; CBSE and CISCE schools follow their own admission tests.

## Reservation

25 per cent of entry-level seats are reserved for children from economically weaker sections under the Right to Education Act.
