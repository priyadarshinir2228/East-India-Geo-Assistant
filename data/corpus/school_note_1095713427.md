# New Barrackpore Colony Boys' High School

New Barrackpore Colony Boys' High School is a WBCHSE school in New Barrackpore, Kolkata. It is located on Jessore Road. The school has 100 seats in Class XI and the 2026 science cut-off is 60%.

Office hours for admission enquiries: 10:00 to 13:00 on working days.
