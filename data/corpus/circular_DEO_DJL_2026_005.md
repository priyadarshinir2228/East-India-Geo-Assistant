# Circular DEO/DJL/2026/005

Issued by: District Education Office, Darjeeling
Date: 12 March 2026
Subject: Revised hill schools admission dates
Applies to: hill schools affiliated to WBBSE and CISCE

This circular supersedes circular DEO/DJL/2026/002.

Because of a landslide on Hill Cart Road, the admission tests are postponed to 21 March 2026 and the new session will begin on 6 April 2026.

Students of Government Boys High School and Turnbull Boys High School who live along Tenzing Norgay Road may attend the test at St. Michael's School in North Point instead.
